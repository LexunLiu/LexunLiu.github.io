# 6. Agile Events

## 6.1 Overview

Agile events provide regular opportunities for planning, collaboration, inspection, and adaptation.

Each event has a clearly defined objective and should maximize value while minimizing unnecessary overhead.

Philips Shanghai R&D Center adopts Scrum events together with selected SAFe program-level events to support collaboration across multiple Scrum Teams.

---

## 6.2 Program Increment (PI) Planning

### Purpose

Program Increment (PI) Planning aligns multiple Agile Teams around a common mission and establishes a shared commitment for the upcoming Program Increment.

### Objectives

- Align business objectives
- Synchronize multiple teams
- Identify dependencies
- Identify risks
- Commit PI Objectives

### Participants

- Business Owner
- Product Manager
- Project Manager
- Release Train Engineer
- Product Owner
- Scrum Master
- Architects
- Development Teams
- QA Engineers

### Inputs

- Product Roadmap
- Feature Backlog
- Architecture Vision
- Capacity Planning
- Previous PI Metrics

### Outputs

- PI Objectives
- Team Objectives
- Program Board
- Dependency List
- Risk Register

### Best Practices

- Prepare backlog before PI Planning
- Ensure all stakeholders participate
- Visualize dependencies
- Focus on business value
- Resolve major risks during planning

---

## 6.3 Sprint Planning

### Purpose

Determine the Sprint Goal and select Stories for implementation.

### Duration

Maximum eight hours for a one-month Sprint.

### Activities

- Review Sprint Goal
- Review prioritized Stories
- Confirm team capacity
- Estimate effort
- Define Sprint Backlog

### Deliverables

- Sprint Goal
- Sprint Backlog
- Sprint Forecast

---

## 6.4 Daily Scrum

### Purpose

Synchronize team activities and identify impediments.

### Duration

15 minutes.

### Discussion Topics

- What has been completed?
- What will be completed today?
- What obstacles exist?

### Expected Outcomes

- Updated Sprint Board
- Identified impediments
- Team synchronization

### Best Practices

- Focus on Sprint Goal
- Keep discussion short
- Escalate detailed discussions
- Update work items immediately

---

## 6.5 Backlog Refinement

### Purpose

Prepare Stories for future Sprints.

### Activities

- Clarify requirements
- Split Stories
- Estimate Story Points
- Identify dependencies
- Update Acceptance Criteria

### Deliverables

- Ready Stories
- Updated Product Backlog

---

## 6.6 Sprint Review

### Purpose

Inspect completed work with stakeholders and collect feedback.

### Participants

- Product Owner
- Scrum Team
- Business Representatives
- Customers
- Stakeholders

### Deliverables

- Accepted Stories
- Customer Feedback
- Updated Product Backlog

### Best Practices

Demonstrate working software instead of presentation slides.

---

## 6.7 Sprint Retrospective

### Purpose

Improve team effectiveness.

### Discussion Topics

- What worked well?
- What should be improved?
- What actions will be taken?

### Deliverables

- Improvement Actions
- Team Commitments
- Retrospective Report

---

## 6.8 Scrum of Scrums

### Purpose

Coordinate multiple Scrum Teams.

### Objectives

- Remove cross-team impediments
- Synchronize dependencies
- Align delivery schedules

### Participants

- Scrum Masters
- Project Manager
- Release Train Engineer
- Architects

### Deliverables

- Dependency Updates
- Risk Updates
- Cross-Team Action Items

---

## 6.9 System Demo

### Purpose

Demonstrate integrated functionality developed by multiple Agile Teams.

### Objectives

- Validate integrated solution
- Gather stakeholder feedback
- Verify business value

### Deliverables

- Demo Feedback
- Improvement Items

---

## 6.10 Inspect & Adapt Workshop

### Purpose

Evaluate Program Increment performance and continuously improve the development process.

### Activities

- Review PI Metrics
- Root Cause Analysis
- Improvement Workshop
- Action Planning

### Deliverables

- Improvement Backlog
- Process Improvement Plan
