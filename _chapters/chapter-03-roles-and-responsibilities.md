# 3. Roles and Responsibilities

## 3.1 Introduction

Successful Agile delivery depends on clear ownership and collaboration.

Each role contributes different expertise while sharing responsibility for delivering customer value.

No single role owns project success.

Project success is achieved through collaboration.

---

# 3.2 Portfolio Roles

## Business Owner

### Purpose

Provide business direction and investment priorities.

### Responsibilities

- Define business objectives
- Prioritize strategic initiatives
- Approve major releases
- Evaluate business value
- Participate in milestone reviews

### Deliverables

- Business Vision
- Roadmap
- Business Value Assessment

---

## Product Management

### Purpose

Define product strategy.

### Responsibilities

- Define Features
- Prioritize Program Backlog
- Coordinate Product Roadmap
- Work with customers
- Support PI Planning

### Deliverables

- Product Vision
- Product Roadmap
- Feature Definition

---

# 3.3 Program Roles

## Project Manager

### Purpose

Ensure successful project execution through planning, coordination, governance, and delivery management.

### Responsibilities

- Project Planning
- Schedule Management
- Risk Management
- Stakeholder Communication
- Resource Coordination
- Budget Tracking
- Delivery Governance
- Escalation Management

### Deliverables

- Project Plan
- RAID Log
- Milestone Plan
- Status Report
- Risk Register

---

## Release Train Engineer (RTE)

### Purpose

Facilitate Agile execution across multiple Scrum Teams.

### Responsibilities

- Facilitate PI Planning
- Remove cross-team impediments
- Track Program progress
- Coordinate dependencies
- Improve Agile maturity

### Deliverables

- PI Objectives
- Program Board
- Dependency List
- ART Metrics

---

## System Architect

### Purpose

Provide technical direction.

### Responsibilities

- Architecture Vision
- Technical Roadmap
- Technology Selection
- Enabler Definition
- Architecture Governance

### Deliverables

- Architecture Document
- Technology Roadmap
- Enabler Backlog

---

# 3.4 Team Roles

### Purpose

Maximize product value.

### Responsibilities

- Manage Product Backlog
- Prioritize Stories
- Define Acceptance Criteria
- Accept completed Stories
- Clarify requirements

### Deliverables

- Product Backlog
- Sprint Goal

## Scrum Master

### Purpose

Improve team effectiveness.

- Remove Impediments

### Deliverables

# 3.6 Role Interaction

The Product Owner focuses on **What** to build.

The Development Team focuses on **How** to build.

The Scrum Master focuses on **How the team works**.

The Project Manager focuses on **How the project succeeds**.

The Architect focuses on **Technical direction**.

Together they ensure successful product delivery.
