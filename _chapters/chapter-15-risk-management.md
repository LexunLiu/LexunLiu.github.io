# 15. Risk Management

## 15.1 Purpose

Risk Management is the continuous process of identifying, assessing, mitigating, monitoring, and communicating risks that may impact product quality, project delivery, business objectives, or customer satisfaction.

Effective risk management enables Agile teams to respond proactively rather than reactively.

Risk management is everyone's responsibility.

---

# 15.2 Risk Management Principles

Philips adopts the following principles:

- Identify risks early.
- Make risks visible.
- Continuously reassess risks.
- Mitigate before escalation.
- Assign ownership.
- Learn from previous projects.

---

# 15.3 Risk Categories

Typical project risks include:

## Business Risks

- Market changes
- Budget reduction
- Customer requirement changes
- Product strategy changes

---

## Project Risks

- Schedule delay
- Resource shortage
- Scope creep
- Vendor dependency

---

## Technical Risks

- New technology
- Legacy integration
- Performance issues
- Security vulnerabilities

---

## Operational Risks

- Infrastructure failure
- Environment instability
- Deployment failure
- Monitoring gaps

---

## Compliance Risks

- Regulatory changes
- Security compliance
- Audit findings
- Licensing issues

---

# 15.4 Risk Assessment

Each risk should be evaluated based on:

- Probability
- Impact
- Exposure
- Urgency

Example Risk Matrix

| Impact | Low | Medium | High |
|---------|------|---------|------|
| High Probability | High | Critical | Critical |
| Medium Probability | Medium | High | Critical |
| Low Probability | Low | Medium | High |

---

# 15.5 Risk Response Strategies

Possible responses include:

- Avoid
- Reduce
- Transfer
- Accept

Every High or Critical risk must have a mitigation plan.

---

# 15.6 Risk Register

Each project should maintain a Risk Register.

Minimum fields include:

- Risk ID
- Description
- Category
- Probability
- Impact
- Owner
- Mitigation Plan
- Due Date
- Current Status

---

# 15.7 Risk Review

Project risks should be reviewed:

- Sprint Planning
- Sprint Review
- PI Planning
- Milestone Reviews
- Steering Committee Meetings

---

# 15.8 Escalation

Risks should be escalated when:

- Business impact increases.
- Delivery commitment is threatened.
- Multiple teams are affected.
- Customer commitments cannot be met.
