# 18. Templates and Best Practices

## 18.1 Purpose

Standardized templates improve consistency, communication, governance, and project efficiency across software development teams.

Project teams are encouraged to tailor templates to their specific needs while maintaining required governance information.

---

# 18.2 Recommended Templates

The following templates are recommended for all software development projects:

### Project Management

- Project Charter
- Project Plan
- Milestone Plan
- RAID Log
- Risk Register
- Stakeholder Register
- Communication Plan

---

### Product Management

- Product Vision
- Product Roadmap
- Feature Definition
- Story Template
- Acceptance Criteria Template

---

### Agile Execution

## 18.3 Best Practices

Recommended practices include:

- Deliver small increments frequently.
- Limit Work in Progress (WIP).
- Build quality into every activity.
- Automate repetitive tasks.
- Visualize work.
- Manage technical debt continuously.
- Measure outcomes, not activity.
- Encourage cross-functional collaboration.
- Continuously improve through feedback.

---

## 18.4 Document Control

All project artifacts should:

- Be version controlled.
- Have an identified owner.
- Include review history.
- Be accessible to relevant stakeholders.
- Follow organizational naming conventions.
