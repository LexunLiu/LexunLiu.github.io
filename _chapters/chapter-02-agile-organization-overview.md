# 2. Agile Organization Overview

## 2.1 Organizational Structure

Philips Shanghai R&D Center adopts a scaled Agile organization that combines Lean thinking, Scrum execution, and enterprise governance.

Software development is organized into three collaboration layers.

```

Portfolio

↓

Program

↓

Team

```

Each layer has different responsibilities while sharing the same objective:

> Deliver customer value continuously.

---

## 2.2 Portfolio Layer

The Portfolio Layer focuses on strategic investment and business objectives.

Primary responsibilities include:

- Product Strategy
- Business Value Definition
- Portfolio Prioritization
- Investment Decisions
- Roadmap Management
- Budget Planning
- Governance

Typical participants include:

- Business Owner
- Product Management
- R&D Director
- Engineering Director

---

## 2.3 Program Layer

The Program Layer coordinates multiple Scrum Teams working toward a common product vision.

Its primary responsibilities include:

- Feature Planning
- Dependency Management
- Architecture Alignment
- PI Planning
- Risk Management
- Release Planning

Typical participants include:

- Product Manager
- Project Manager
- Release Train Engineer
- System Architect
- Product Owner
- Scrum Master

---

## 2.4 Team Layer

The Team Layer focuses on delivering working software.

Each Scrum Team is cross-functional and self-organizing.

Typical activities include:

- Sprint Planning
- Daily Scrum
- Story Development
- Testing
- Sprint Review
- Sprint Retrospective

---

## 2.5 Organizational Principles

The Philips Agile organization follows these principles.

### Customer Value

Every activity should contribute customer value.

---

### Alignment

All teams work toward shared business objectives.

---

### Built-in Quality

Quality is integrated into every engineering activity.

---

### Transparency

Project information should be visible across the organization.

---

### Continuous Improvement

Every Sprint should improve both product and process.
